# Primary 6 Reading Skills — Week 2

## Scope
Build and publish only Week 2 inside the existing paid Primary 6 Reading Skills course. Week 1, enrollment, authentication, payment, and unrelated pages remain unchanged.

## Implementation
- Add Lesson 2, **Finding the Main Idea**, to the existing course with the supplied title, introduction, objectives, video, teaching notes, vocabulary, and 40–50 minute estimate.
- Add a Week 2-only lesson presentation that reuses the current lesson page and shared video, quiz, assignment, progress, and download components.
- Add **The School Garden** reading practice with four instant-feedback comprehension questions.
- Add the four-step **Main Idea Check** strategy and a five-item **Main Idea or Detail?** activity.
- Persist the Main Idea activity responses securely against the signed-in student and Lesson 2; use them as one of the real completion requirements.
- Generate one downloadable **Week 2 — Main Idea & Supporting Details Worksheet** containing six questions and attach it through the existing materials system.
- Add the five-question auto-graded Week 2 quiz with a 4/5 pass mark, saved attempts, clear score, completion, review, and retry states.
- Add the **My Main Idea** assignment through the existing submission and teacher-review flow, supporting a written response and/or the current accepted uploads.
- Gate **Mark lesson complete** on the actual Week 2 requirements, then save completion through the existing lesson progress system.
- Update the existing course dashboard to show Week 1 and Week 2 from real course data. Do not add or link to Week 3.

## Verification
- Confirm enrolled students can open both published weeks without another payment, while unenrolled students remain locked.
- Test Lesson 2 on desktop and phone for video sizing, reading layout, activities, worksheet, quiz, assignment, completion, progress, and return navigation.
- Confirm Week 1 content and behavior are unchanged and no Week 3 lesson exists.
